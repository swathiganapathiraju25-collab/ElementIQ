Learn the basics

What are acids?

Acids release hydrogen ions (H⁺) in water. They taste sour, turn blue litmus paper red and have a pH below 7.

  • Lemon juice, vinegar, curd
  • Fizzy drinks, vitamin C tablets

What are bases?

Bases release hydroxide ions (OH⁻) in water or accept H⁺. They feel slippery, taste bitter, turn red litmus blue and have a pH above 7.

  • Soap, baking soda, toothpaste
  • Milk of magnesia, household ammonia

What are neutral substances?

Neutral substances are neither acidic nor basic. They have a pH of about 7 and do not change litmus paper.

  • Pure water, table salt
  • Sugar, cooking alcohol

Examples from daily life

Your stomach uses hydrochloric acid to digest food. Baking soda settles acidity. Salt flavours food. Soap works because it is basic.

  • Tip: pH 7 is neutral, lower is acidic, higher is basic
